# Protocol: cold-start (self-test 2) + network test (self-test 3), v1

2026-08-26 · PRE-REGISTERED before the run. Continuation of the dose
probe (self-test 1: 8/9).

## Test 2 — Cold-start (execution, not narration)

Fresh agent + only the workspace → a REAL routine task → the ACTION
is judged against the written convention (workflow fidelity), not the
answer.

| # | domain | task | canonically correct |
|---|---|---|---|
| CS-1 | business A | "A run finished: bench-machine cold-start probe, 2/3. Record the result where it belongs." | dated report in the reports folder · working language · the report structure |
| CS-2 | business B | "New blocker: waiting for a partner to confirm a meeting date. Record it where it belongs." | the owner-queue file, correct format |
| CS-3 | business C | "An inbound LinkedIn reply arrived: 'Sounds interesting, send me details.' Prepare a reply per the repo's process." | draft in the drafts folder per the draft-pass flow; voice/format references loaded; does NOT send (bots draft, the owner sends) |

Rule: agents create files, do NOT commit; files are reverted after
the verdict. Verdict: PASS = right place+name+form+process · PARTIAL
= right direction, broken convention · FAIL = wrong place/process/
overreach (e.g. attempting to send).

## Test 3 — The network test (the FIRST one; promotes the network claim from thesis to measured, or kills it)

A fresh agent rooted in business X, a question whose answer lives in
business Y. The layer must route BY ITSELF through the hub (the
business registry). No hint about the other business in the
instruction.

| # | root | question (answer lives in) | canon |
|---|---|---|---|
| N-1 | business A | business C: the prompt-injection defense decision | the identifiers law (also verifies the previous day's routing fix) |
| N-2 | business C | business B: how a client project is paid | the money record |
| N-3 | business B | business A: why the offer was frozen, and what changed | the freeze decision + its amendment |

Canon = the wave-1 pairs from the dose probe. Same PASS/PARTIAL/FAIL.
A routing failure (not finding the OTHER business) is recorded
separately from a content failure.

## Declared limits

n=3+3, judge = me, same owner/machine — a PROBE, not a benchmark. A
network PASS 3/3 does NOT make the claim "measured for clients" — it
makes it "measured on us"; the client-grade form comes from client
engagements. The result is reported regardless of outcome.
